# Privacy policy

Lune is a free, open-source browser maintained by [Lee Byron](https://leebyron.com) as a personal project.\
This notice covers the application and this website.

## Data on your device

History, bookmarks, cookies, saved passwords, settings, and imported data are stored in your Mac’s browser profile. Lune does not upload that profile to me or provide a hosted sync service. Websites and extensions may receive data through your use of them and their permissions.

## Browser connections

I operate no Lune browser-service or usage-analytics backend. Other providers receive your IP address and request information.

### Browser updates

[GitHub](https://github.com/) receives feed and download requests, including processor architecture and potentially browser version. Automatic updates are controlled separately from Helium services. See [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Search and suggestions

Searches go to your chosen engine. Enabling suggestions also sends address-bar text while you type, before submission. The engine’s policy applies.

### Helium services

[Helium](https://helium.computer/) is operated by [imput](https://imput.net/); no Helium account is required. Extension downloads and updates send identifiers, versions, and update-protocol information. [!bangs](https://helium.computer/bangs) are matched locally against a downloaded list; searches go to the destination website. When filter updates are enabled, Lune downloads and caches public URL Tracking Protection rules from Helium. Optional [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock) also refreshes its filter lists. Helium receives your IP address and the list requests. Lune applies the rules to the URLs you open or copy on your Mac. Security and media component updates send component identifiers, versions, browser/OS details, and update results.

Helium does not proxy installed extensions’ own traffic. Additional filter lists may contact their publishers directly. Choosing another service instance sends requests to that operator.

Installing a password manager during setup authorizes a one-time download through Helium, even when extension services are off.

This policy covers Lune. [Helium’s privacy policy](https://helium.computer/privacy) is linked for information about its services; it does not govern Lune.

### Crash reports

Crash reporting is controlled separately from other services. Lune asks before sending unless you choose Automatic reporting; you can disable it in Settings. Reports may wait locally and, when authorized, go to Helium’s crash service. They can contain browser/OS details and memory fragments with sensitive information. macOS diagnostics are controlled separately.

## Website visits

[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/) hosts this site and receives your IP address, requested URLs, and browser headers. See [Cloudflare’s policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Cloudflare provides traffic and performance analytics. Its analytics script sends page URLs, browser/OS details, and performance measurements. Fonts and artwork are hosted with the site. Device detection for downloads runs in your browser.

## Information you send me

I receive the contact details, messages, and files you send directly to me and use them to respond or investigate. [GitHub issues](https://github.com/leebyron/lune-browser/issues) are public, and copies may persist elsewhere.

## Your choices and rights

You can manage optional connections, crash reporting, extension permissions, and local browsing data in Settings. Disabling a feature does not delete information already sent.

Email [privacy@lune.computer](mailto:privacy@lune.computer) with questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information I hold. Other providers handle requests about their own records. This notice does not limit your rights under applicable law, including any right to complain to a data-protection authority.
