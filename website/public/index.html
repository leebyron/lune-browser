LuneHeliumLune on GitHubLee Byron

A browser should respect your privacy, your attention, and the way you use your Mac.

Your browsing belongs to you, so Lune leaves Google’s background services behind.

Ads and trackers get in your way, so uBlock Origin is built in.

Encrypted connections and site isolation help protect what you read and share.

A Mac browser should feel at home, with native controls and the keyboard shortcuts you already know.

Your tabs and familiar Mac controls stay close when you need them and step aside when you don’t.

You choose whether to use Helium’s optional services, and Lune asks for your consent.

Lune’s source and browser changes are public, so you can see what goes into the browser you use.
