Security

A little peace of mind.

A Chromium foundation, carefully shaped by ungoogled-chromium and Helium.

Encrypted connections
Helium favors HTTPS, keeping the connection between you and a website encrypted.
Sites kept apart
Chromium’s sandbox and site isolation help keep a page from reaching beyond its boundaries.
Open to inspection
From ungoogled-chromium’s patches to Helium’s browser and services, the code is there to read.
Privacy

Leave a lighter trace.

Lune carries forward the privacy work of ungoogled-chromium and Helium.

Less following you around
Helium’s built-in uBlock Origin filters ads, trackers, and cookie banners. Third-party cookies are blocked by default.
A softer fingerprint
Helium adds noise to browser signals, making you harder to track across sites.
Fewer calls home
ungoogled-chromium removes Google service dependencies and their background connections.
