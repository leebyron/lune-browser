I made Lune for myself. I wanted a private browser that stayed out of my way.

Lune starts with up-to-date Chromium.

Ads and trackers are blocked from the start, with uBlock Origin.

Third-party cookies are blocked, so fewer sites can follow you around.

Google’s background services are removed.

Optional services stay off until you say yes.

Chrome extension downloads and updates are proxied, so Google can’t track them.

Find tabs, history, and search in one place with ⌘T.

Tuck the sidebar away when you just want the page.

Bring your bookmarks and choose your own search engine.

Lune is open source, built on Helium and ungoogled-chromium.
